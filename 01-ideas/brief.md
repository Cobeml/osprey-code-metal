# Hackathon project brief

Recorded 2026-10-29 from Colton's request and the attached equipment inventory. Event timing: **next week**; exact date, venue, team size and judging criteria remain unknown.

## Mission supplied by Colton

> The mission: help locate people, detect hazards, and get critical information to responders when infrastructure goes down. Start thinking about what you could build—and what could make a difference when every second counts.

## Confirmed equipment access

The [organizer PDF](../02-equipment/source/Hackathon%20Hardware%20Details.pdf) says every team has access to:

- Raspberry Pi 5 with 16 GB RAM, a 26 TOPS AI accelerator, storage and power supply.
- An Arduino-compatible microcontroller with Grove expansion shield.
- A Grove 40-in-1 sensor and actuator kit.
- Development accessories for circuits, sensors and hardware connections.

The additional computers, cameras, thermal equipment, distance sensors, localization hardware, radios, radar, environmental/audio sensors, robots, drones, motors, prototyping tools, test equipment, assembly supplies and displays are a **shared checkout library**. The PDF does not give exact models, quantities or reservations. It does not identify the 40 Grove modules or guarantee a camera per team.

The accelerator's 26 TOPS rating is consistent with a Hailo-8 AI HAT+, but that identity remains an inference. Do not select its driver, compiler or model format until the label and supplied OS image are checked.

## Decisions and proposals

Confirmed: keep the project here, preserve the equipment source, provide a beginner guide and crash course, research preparation tools, and create a generic Git repository for later coding.

Proposed: use the guaranteed kit for an offline incident board; begin with Python, Arduino firmware, USB serial and local storage. Add one specialist capability after a working end-to-end demo exists. These are recommendations, not a chosen product or approved implementation.
