# Maritime search and rescue demo

November 7, 2026 at 11:37 AM EST. Curated, paraphrased team notes based on the complete four-part transcript. [Original Granola meeting](https://notes.granola.ai/d/1f63321e-6bd6-4278-b492-ac985c9c4e4c)

## Proposed demonstration

Start with a manual MOB button. A person represents the target and one or more camera nodes search for and observe them. The first sighting is shared with other nodes and a command display. The display shows positions, last observations and a growing uncertainty area when updates stop.

Participants discussed handheld phone cameras as an available demonstration approach, a quadcopter if accessible, and a robot as a possible additional element. Thermal input was desirable, with visible-light computer vision as an alternative.

## Scope discussion

The group supported a manual trigger for initial scope. A hull-camera threshold event was discussed as a later extension. Rapid acquisition and continued awareness remained the goal. Node coordination, actual flight and robot motion were possibilities requiring further decisions.

A venue contact offered to investigate quadcopter access. Staff operation was discussed. Robot availability was reported tentatively. These remarks do not establish checked-out hardware or working interfaces.

## Software and integration discussion

The team reviewed Pi detection examples, Roboflow tracking and annotations, Stone Soup, OpenDrift, SeaDronesSee and MAVSDK. It proposed connecting available devices to a development machine and iterating with coding assistants after agreeing on a PRD.

The intended workflow was a collaborative PRD interview, shared repository, implementation and frequent team review. No implementation owners or complete stack were assigned.

## Demo checkpoints discussed

1. Acquire and track a target in live video or labeled replay.
2. Send location and evidence periodically.
3. Show the age of the ship's information and estimated search-region growth.
4. Restore communications and recover the latest contact.
5. Hide the target while keeping communications active to show the different failure state.

## Engineering corrections and open issues

- The transcript's library name resembling Heliopy appears to refer to the previously discussed Hailo Pi examples. The project shortlist identifies the actual Hailo repository and keeps hardware compatibility conditional.
- LoRa and Wi-Fi communications do not by themselves establish accurate triangulation or relative positioning.
- Sending stored packets after reconnection differs from transmitting across a completely unavailable link.
- Aircraft telemetry, camera calibration, power and SDK access remain unresolved.
- The team needs an explicit definition of live, replay and simulated evidence for each demo component.
